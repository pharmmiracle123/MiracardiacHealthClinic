FAQs

How do I consult a doctor?

Go to Find a Doctor, select a doctor, pay the consultation fee. After admin approval your chat opens for 7 days.

How long does payment approval take?

Usually within a few hours during business hours. You will be notified when the chat is activated.

Can I get a refund?

If a consultation is rejected by admin or the doctor is unavailable, you may request a refund via a support ticket.

How do pharmacy orders work?

Search a drug, choose a pharmacy, add to cart, checkout with pickup or delivery. Track status live.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Only you, your assigned doctor and platform admins can access your health records. We comply with NDPR.
