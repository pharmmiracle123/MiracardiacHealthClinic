Frequently asked questions

How do I start chatting with a doctor?⌄

Pick a doctor, pay the consultation fee, and wait for approval. Once approved, the chat opens in your account.

How long does approval take?⌄

Our team reviews payments as quickly as possible. If you wait too long, contact Support from the app.

Can I buy prescription drugs?⌄

Yes. Prescription-only drugs need a valid prescription from a doctor or an uploaded prescription.

I am a doctor or pharmacist. Can I join?⌄

Yes. Register as a doctor or join as a pharmacy. Your account is activated after verification.
